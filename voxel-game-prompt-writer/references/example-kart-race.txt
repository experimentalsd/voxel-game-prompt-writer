Voxel Kart Cup
Build a single-file HTML5 game, "Voxel Kart Cup": a six-kart, 3-lap race in pixel-art 3D on a floating voxel island. three.js r128 from cdnjs, everything else inline, no asset files, no build step for the player. three.js and Google Fonts need internet: load cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js, on error the same build from cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.128.0/build/three.min.js, and if both fail show "three.js could not load - check your connection" in the stage; fonts fall back to system stacks.

ARCHITECTURE

* Split sim from render. All game logic is one plain-JS sim in its own <script> block: no DOM, no THREE, no Date, no Math.random (seeded mulberry32), fixed step 1/120 s. API: createSim(seed, mode, level, laps), sim.step(), sim.input(slot, intent) with intent = steer, throttle, brake, drift; sim.state is plain data; sim.events feeds sounds, captions and log. AI and humans write the same intents.
* The renderer reads state, interpolates the last two steps and never writes it. Accumulator loop, at most 8 steps per frame; game speed multiplies steps, not dt. Every phase has a maximum duration and a fallback transition.
* Expose window.GAME (createSim, runHeadless(seconds, opts) returning stats); the sim block must run unchanged in Node via vm, so tests step thousands of seconds without a browser.

LOOK

* Render to a low-res target (2 px art pixels on wide stages, 1 px on phones; slider 1-6; NearestFilter, CSS image-rendering: pixelated), then a post pass: depth-based outlines, snap every pixel to one fixed 90-colour OKLab palette, ordered 4x4 Bayer dither. Orthographic camera with smooth azimuth / elevation / zoom, pixel-snapped in view space so voxels never shimmer. Sun shadow map; Day / Dusk / Night / Auto lighting. Eight lamp posts (hairpin, chicane, gantry) must be registered as point lights BEFORE the lights are initialised, otherwise night is pitch black; no point-light shadows. r128 has no CapsuleGeometry and no core mergeBufferGeometries; use renderer.outputEncoding.
* Island: a 130 x 85 m slab of 1 m voxel columns with a rounded outline plus a 30 x 22 m satellite joined by a wooden bridge; grassy top (three tones from noise), layered rock strata underneath, floating rocks; solid through the centre from any angle. A ground canvas paints the 9 m asphalt track (640 m lap), red-white kerbs, chequered start line, six grid boxes, sand traps.
* Track: start straight under a gantry with start lights; a timing tower with a canvas scoreboard (positions in kart colours, lap n/3, best lap; redrawn only on change); hairpin, S chicane, the bridge with a 0.6 m ramp lip, a rock tunnel, four boost pads, tyre walls, twelve invisible checkpoint gates.
* Surroundings: two tiered wooden grandstands (straight, hairpin) with an instanced crowd in the six kart colours who jump more at overtakes and the finish; pit garages, juice stand, marshal posts, palms, gulls, clouds in the sky shader.
* Doll's-house lift: grandstands, timing tower and tunnel rock are lift groups; a group the sight line from camera target to camera crosses below roof height slides up in 0.4 s (bounding-box test) and stops casting shadows; option "Stands lift away". Merge static meshes by material with a small inline merge (bake matrixWorld, concatenate attributes, castShadow after merging); karts, flags and start lights stay separate.

KARTS AND DRIVERS

* Karts from boxes: tray, seat, engine, bumpers, four wheels on axle groups (fronts steer, all spin), canvas number plate. Six teams, one colour each, distinct after palette snapping: Coral, Lagoon, Mango, Basalt, Lime, Orchid.
* Drivers: jointed box rigs (hips, torso, helmeted head, upper arms and forearms on shoulder and elbow groups, hands on the wheel, tucked legs). A pose function per state returns channels (torso lean / twist, head yaw / pitch, shoulders (pitch, roll), elbows, wheel angle, bounce): grid idle (rev nods), steer (lean in), drift (counter-steer, head to apex), boost (tuck), bump flinch, spin-out wobble, airborne, celebrate (stand, fist pump), sad (slump); marshals wave green, yellow and chequered flags. Keyframed ease-in-out, then a critically damped spring per channel so poses never snap. Four suspension springs keep the wheels on the track.

DRIVING PHYSICS

* Integrate at 120 Hz on the track plane with heights from a heightfield; gravity 9.81 when airborne. Mass 150 kg, top speed 20 m/s, 0-20 m/s in 3.2 s, braking 12 m/s2, rolling resistance 0.4 m/s2, drag scaled so 20 m/s is the limit. Grass caps speed at 9 m/s, sand at 5 m/s.
* Steering: bicycle model, wheelbase 1.05 m, max steer 28 degrees falling to 9 at top speed; lateral grip 1.3 g on asphalt, 0.7 g on grass; beyond it the kart slides, lateral velocity decaying at 6 per s.
* Drift: hold drift while steering above 10 m/s: grip 0.9 g, yaw 20-35 degrees into the turn, charge +1 per s; release at 1.0 for +4 m/s over 0.8 s, at 2.0 for +6 m/s over 1.2 s (blue, then orange sparks).
* Boost pad +6 m/s for 1.0 s, cap 26 m/s. Slipstream: 1.5 s inside a 6 m, 15 degree cone behind a kart gives +1.5 m/s.
* Karts are 1.4 x 0.9 m oriented boxes, restitution 0.3; walls 0.2 with 30% speed loss head-on; sweep against walls so nothing tunnels at 26 m/s. Off the bridge edge: fall, respawn at the last checkpoint after 2.5 s.

RACE RULES

* 3 laps (selectable 1 / 3 / 5); first grid random, then reversed results. Three red lights 1 s apart, then green; throttle in the last 0.3 s gives a start boost (+3 m/s), earlier throttle stalls for 0.6 s.
* A lap counts only after all 12 checkpoints in order. Positions from laps, checkpoint index and distance to the next gate, every step. Driving backwards for 3 s shows WRONG WAY.
* The race ends 20 s after the winner or when all finish; the rest are ranked by progress. Points 10 / 8 / 6 / 4 / 2 / 1; a cup is 4 races, then a new cup starts (endless).
* Calls: GO!, START BOOST, STALLED, OVERTAKE, NEW LEADER, FASTEST LAP, BIG DRIFT, SLIPSTREAM, SPIN OUT, OFF THE EDGE, WRONG WAY, FINAL LAP, PHOTO FINISH (gap under 0.1 s), WINNER. After the finish: chequered flag, top three celebrate, the rest slump, next grid 6 s later.

DRIVING AND CONTACT

* Bump: side contact above 3 m/s relative speed yaws the hit kart 15 degrees with a flinch; above 8 m/s it is a SPIN OUT: 1.2 s spin, speed down to 30%.
* Effects: grass flecks, sand puffs, skid marks (400-segment ring buffer), wall sparks.
* Levels Easy / Normal / Pro: AI top speed 92 / 97 / 100%, line error 1.2 / 0.6 / 0.25 m, drift use 30 / 70 / 95%, reaction 0.5 / 0.35 / 0.2 s, late braking at the hairpin 8 / 4 / 1% per lap. Rubber band on Easy and Normal only: AI behind the leading human +4 / +2% top speed, ahead -4 / -2%.

AI (all karts not driven by a human)

* Precompute a racing line: spline through the apexes, target speed 0.95 x sqrt(grip x g x radius), braking points from a 40 m look-ahead at 12 m/s2.
* Pure-pursuit steering with a look-ahead of 6 + 0.4 x speed metres, plus a per-driver lateral offset from smooth noise (scaled by level) so karts do not stack. Overtake: a slower kart within 8 m ahead triggers a 1.6 m shift to the roomier side, back after passing.
* Drift where the radius is under 18 m above 12 m/s, release at charge 1.0 or at the corner exit; take boost pads within 2 m of the line; draft on straights.
* Recover: under 1 m/s for 1.5 s, reverse 1 s with opposite steer. Never drive the wrong way for more than 1 s. Never read human input.

HUMAN CONTROL

* Modes: Watch (all six on autopilot, the default), One player (Coral), Two players on one keyboard (Coral and Lagoon). A human slot overrides the autopilot for its own kart only.
* P1: W / S throttle / brake-reverse, A / D steer, Left Shift drift; P2: arrows, Right Shift drift; one player accepts both sets. Read e.code, preventDefault Space and arrows, blur panel buttons after a click so Space never re-presses them. During the countdown only throttle counts. An arrow bobs over each human kart.
* Touch: a steering pad plus Gas / Brake / Drift buttons in a dock below the stage, only on (pointer: coarse); pointer capture, touch-action: none.

CAMERAS

* Director (default): grid flyover in the countdown, chase cam on the leader or the human, trackside cut for overtakes within 30 m of a TV point, close on the winner, island orbit between races; in Two players it frames both humans. Fixed: Chase, Trackside TV, Hairpin, Bridge, Top-down, Island. Zoom in metres of visible width, aspect-corrected. Drag to turn, wheel / pinch to zoom, Q / E rotate, 1-6 pick, L light, P pause, N new race, M sound; a user drag pauses the director for 4 s.

UI (CSS tokens on :root, dark scheme; fonts: Bungee, Barlow Condensed, Public Sans, system fallbacks)

* Masthead with race n/4 and the cup leader; a framed stage with the race bug (six rows: position, swatch, name, gap, lap), lap timer and best lap, drift charge bar for humans, call-out captions, chips (paused / speed / camera / quality tier) and the latest log line. Control panel on the right (below on phones): mode, level, laps, speed 0.5x / 1x / 2x / 4x, New race / Pause; how-to-play; camera, light, turn / zoom, pixel size, Outlines / Dither / Raw 3D / Sound / Stands lift; cup stats (wins, podiums, fastest laps, overtakes, spin-outs, best lap, points); race log; a "How it's drawn" note with the palette swatches. No horizontal scroll at 390 px.
* Sound: Web Audio API only, off by default; create or resume the AudioContext in the click that enables it, otherwise it stays silent. Engines: sawtooth plus square oscillators, pitch 80-260 Hz with speed, 3 nearest karts only; drift screech (band-passed noise), boost whoosh (rising noise sweep), bump clunk, wall thud, countdown beeps (440 Hz three times, 880 Hz on green), lap chime, crowd cheer, wind ambience, an occasional gull.

SCOPE

* MUST: sim / render split, rendering pipeline, island and track, six karts with drivers and poses, physics, all rules and calls, AI on every level, Watch + One player, director and fixed cameras, panel, stats, log, sound.
* IF ROOM, only after every MUST works, cut in reverse order: Two players, touch dock, skid marks, tunnel lift, marshal flags, gulls, clouds, Auto light. Never cut a MUST for an IF ROOM item.

PERFORMANCE

* Target 30 fps at 2 px on a mid-range laptop with integrated graphics, 30 fps at 1 px on a recent phone. Budget: under 150 draw calls, InstancedMesh crowd, one 2048 shadow map (1024 on phones), at most 8 point lights. Below 28 fps over 2 s, step down: shadow map 1024 -> crowd every other frame -> no skid marks or sparks -> art pixel +1 -> shadows off; show the tier in a chip. Never slow the sim.

QUALITY BAR

* Node tests, seeds 1-50: 50 races per level in Watch, 20 per level in each human mode with a scripted bot on sim.input. Assert: no NaN or Infinity, no stuck phase (countdown 4 s, race 6 min, podium 8 s), no lap without all checkpoints in order, positions always a permutation of 1-6, and the same seed gives the same final state hash.
* Targets on Normal: average lap 36-46 s, winner-to-last gap 4-15 s, at least 6 overtakes and 0.5-3 spin-outs per race, under 0.2 AI falls per race. Out of range: tune grip, look-ahead, line error and rubber band, rerun; after 3 rounds report the measured values instead of hiding them.
* Browser: no console errors on load and after 60 s of Watch; every control and key works; touch dock only on coarse pointers; no horizontal scroll at 390 px; fps measured or marked unmeasured. Headless software GL is too slow for real-time checks: the renderer gets an error check and a screenshot only. Scan the file for personal data (emails, absolute paths) and broken characters before publishing.
